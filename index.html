Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Lunara stores all data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.

Information You Store

Lunara records only what you choose to enter: peptide and supplement protocols, menstrual cycle phases, weight, skin actives, progress photos, and reminders you set. This information stays on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else.

Permissions

Lunara requests notification access to deliver the reminders you configure, and photo library access if you choose to attach progress photos. Neither permission is used for any purpose beyond that stated function.

Data Storage and Backups

Your data is stored locally using your device's on-device storage. Automatic cloud backup of app data is disabled on Android. If you delete the app, your data is deleted with it. Lunara has no server, account system, or cloud sync of any kind.

Third-Party Services

Lunara does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising services, or any third-party SDKs. The app makes no network requests.

Payments

Lunara does not currently offer in-app purchases. If Lunara introduces an optional one-time purchase in the future, it will be processed entirely through Apple's App Store or Google Play's official payment systems. Lunara will never receive, store, or have access to your payment information — the only data shared is what Apple or Google requires to complete and validate the transaction.

Children's Privacy

Lunara is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating this page.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@lunara.app.
